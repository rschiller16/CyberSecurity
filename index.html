Everyday cybersecurity issues

Phishing emails and texts

A delivery text says your package is on hold and asks for a small fee. Open the carrier's app to check the shipment before entering payment information. See a phishing example from a campus inbox.

Reused passwords

If a shopping site leaks a password you also use for email, someone can try it on your inbox. Give each account its own password. See how two-factor authentication protects a sign-in.

Unexpected attachments

An invoice for a purchase you don't recognize may contain harmful software. Leave the file closed and check your order history through the store's website.

Missed software updates

Putting off a browser or phone update can leave a known security flaw open. Turn on automatic updates in the device or browser settings.
