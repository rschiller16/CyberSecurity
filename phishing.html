Spot A Phishing Email

A phishing email pretends to come from someone you trust. Its goal might be to get you to open a link and enter your password.

Potential Phising Email

From: Campus IT Desk (help@CampuslT.PurdueEDU)

Subject: Dont lose Access!

Boilermaker,

Due to a upcoming change in software used by the Purdue IT Department, students will loose access to their email and Brightspace access if they do not register by 10PM. Use the link before 10 P.M. today to keep your access!

Purdue Student Access Update

Boiler Up!

If you receive a message like this, open your usual campus portal or call IT using the number in the school directory. Report the message using your school's phishing-report option.

Read the FTC guide to phishing

Three warning signs

  • Sender: The I in the email is a lowercase L “Campus IT Purdue EDU”. Compare the email address with the school directory.
  • Deadline: The threat of losing the ability to use required rescourses gives you little time to think.
  • Link: “Purdue Student Access Update” hides the destination. Check the claim through your usual campus portal.
